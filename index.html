About

I am an operations and analytics professional with over eight years of experience leading service delivery and data-driven improvement in Bangladesh's mobile-health sector. I am now preparing for graduate study in digital business and AI management.

My career began in frontline telesales at Milvik Bangladesh in 2017 and progressed into operational leadership, where I spent seven years managing mHealth service delivery — capacity planning, cross-functional coordination, performance reporting, and liaison with local and global technology teams. Working in a resource-constrained healthcare environment showed me both how much well-run operations matter and how often they are constrained by fragmented data and instinct-led decisions.

That experience led me to data analytics. I learned Python and SQL to automate the manual reporting I had been producing by hand, and later, as Senior Executive for Data Analytics, I built the reporting pipelines and KPI dashboards my teams used to make decisions. Leading that transition — from manual reports to automated workflows colleagues trusted — clarified the question that now drives my academic interest: how can analytics and AI improve operational decision-making in resource-constrained settings, and what governance and human oversight make those systems trustworthy?

I am seeking a master's programme in digital business and AI management where I can study these questions rigorously and apply them to organisations in Bangladesh and the wider region, where better-run operations translate directly into better services for people.

Research Interests

My research interest centres on how analytics and AI can improve operational decision-making in resource-constrained service organisations, with attention to trust, adoption, and accountability.

Human-AI Collaboration in Decision-Making

How can machine learning support operational judgment without replacing the domain expertise that makes it trustworthy?

Technology Adoption in Resource-Constrained Healthcare

What makes technology adoption succeed in healthcare and SME contexts, where resources are thin and the stakes are human?

Design of Business Intelligence Systems

How do we design KPI and reporting systems that teams actually use — not just ones that look good in a slide deck?

Governance in Automated Workflows

As workflows automate, how do organisations keep human oversight and accountability where they matter most?
