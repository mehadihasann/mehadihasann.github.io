About

I spent seven years running mHealth operations in Dhaka - reporting, capacity planning, and coordinating teams where reliable numbers kept services moving. That work pulled me into data: I learned Python and SQL to automate the reporting I used to do by hand, then spent a year as a data analytics executive building the dashboards my teams decided from.

The pattern I kept seeing - good operations held back by disconnected data and manual decisions - is what I want to study properly. My goal is a master's in digital business and AI management, focused on how analytics and AI reshape operational decision-making.
